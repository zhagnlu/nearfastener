Our Story

Near Fastener was established in 1991 with a focus on bolt manufacturing. Our factory is located in Shiqiu Industrial Zone, Lishui District, Nanjing City, Jiangsu Province, China. Bolts are at the heart of our business, with a broad product offering for construction, machinery, and general industrial use.

Our story began in 1991, and bolt manufacturing remains our core business today. From our base in Nanjing, we export to over 50 countries, helping customers discuss the products and specifications their projects require. Looking ahead, our aim is to build lasting relationships through clear communication, dependable products, and attentive service.
