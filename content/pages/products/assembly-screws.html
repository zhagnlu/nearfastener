SEMS Screws: Washer Configuration and Factory Ordering

Specify the complete assembly

SEMS ordering starts with the screw head, drive, thread and retained washer configuration. State whether you need a flat washer, a spring washer or a combination. Include washer inside diameter, outside diameter, thickness and order in the assembly.

Sources: PENCOM — machine screw and SEMS configurations

Check the installed envelope

Check the space occupied by the head and washers, tool access and usable thread length against the mating parts. Retaining the washer helps keep components together during handling; it does not by itself establish resistance to loosening in your application.
